This directory houses the HTML5 video files for the homepage "Top Picks" 
3D Cover-Flow Carousel.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. RECOMMENDED VIDEO SPECIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Container Format: MP4 (.mp4)
- Video Codec: H.264 (Baseline or Main profile for universal iOS/Android/Desktop support)
- Resolution: 1080x1350 px (4:5) or 1080x1920 px (9:16 portrait)
- Target File Size: 2 MB – 5 MB per video (optimized for instant web streaming)
- Duration: 6 to 15 seconds seamless loop
- Audio: Muted or none (inline autoplay requires silent/muted video)
- Frame Rate: 24 fps or 30 fps
